Lines Tracker - design ideas

What we have now, then three ways the band could go, a richer pane, and some extras. These are look-only sketches. The ideas came from other mods: savvy-progress (pixel crab with hats per level, twinkling bar), skins (rings, thin-border cards), filetree (hover buttons). Use the buttons; switch light and dark to check both themes.

  • Outfits unlock with levels. You can also pick one with /linecount outfit crown.
