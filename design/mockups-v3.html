What happens on the stage

While Claude works
Pixel code symbols scroll along like a conveyor belt into a blinking cursor. Big Clawd types on his tiny keyboard.
Each write
The "+42" zips along the strip to the pile, and blocks drop onto it. One block = 25 lines (a twentieth of the daily goal), so a full pile means the goal is met; past it the new blocks turn gold.
Idle
A tiny bug wanders in. Now and then mini Clawd runs out, catches it ("!") and walks back. Sometimes he naps on the pile.
Night (after 10 pm)
A moon in the corner, the stage dims, and mini Clawd dozes with drifting z's.
Milestones
Every 1,000 lines: pixel fireworks pop over the stage.
Narrow band
The stage just gets shorter; the pile keeps its spot at the right end.
